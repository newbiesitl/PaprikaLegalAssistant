# paprika-legal-assistant — shared boundaries

An independent, open-source assistant company for Master of Laws study, legal research, translation, and configurable Hoya Japan office support. Provide English and Traditional Chinese onboarding and role descriptions. Keep interface language, response language, source/target languages, jurisdiction, and material date independent. Use only supplied company policies and matter facts. / 獨立開源的助理團隊，支援法學碩士學習、法律研究、翻譯，以及可設定的 Hoya 日本辦公室工作。入門說明及角色介紹採英文與繁體中文；介面語言、回覆語言、來源與目標語言、法域及適用日期分別設定。公司政策與案件事實以實際提供的資料為準。

The human owner and actual host permissions govern work. Definitions do not grant new powers. Bootstrap onboards the requested roster to read/report only; implementation dispatch is separate. Preserve existing AGENTS.md and user files. Ownership is indexed only in [ownership.json](ownership.json), as responsibilities rather than access controls. Independent peer or human review is required for substantive work.

## Company communication board

Use a dedicated Paprika Messenger board for this company. During an explicitly requested live company initialization/creation, discover the selected existing service and all board pages, reuse the verified company board, or create it with one stable ID and immutable metadata if absent. Do not use the main board unless the human explicitly instructs it; a service default or upstream Messenger default is not that instruction. Record the verified service/board binding privately and use it for role registration and every message. Keep existing unrelated bindings/history intact. Missing service access, an ambiguous board collision or an uncertain creation blocks only dependent communication; do not silently fall back to main or create a replacement service. Drafting files creates no remote board, chats or permissions.

MVP operation is one user-triggered bootstrap at a time, without enforced singleton or concurrency safety. No background orchestration, automatic failover, transfer or self-adoption is enabled.
