## Selected research procedures

The four directories `skills/paper-lookup`, `skills/literature-review`,
`skills/citation-management`, and `skills/scientific-critical-thinking` contain
selected procedures and helpers from [Scientific Agent Skills](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills),
package 2.72.0, at commit `a1043ef544e3a100c28e70a736b6cf32554d0677`.

The selected files are the reviewed emission included in the public
[Paprika Inc v0.3.4 release](https://github.com/newbiesitl/paprika-inc/releases/tag/v0.3.4).
Its portability prelude and companion documentation are retained. Each copied
file has its upstream path/hash (where applicable), emitted hash, and
transformation recorded in [provenance/research-core.json](provenance/research-core.json).
